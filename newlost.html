Chapter 1 · 2:17 a.m.

The Call

The Lost & Found Bureau never sleeps. It just loses track of the alarm clock.

At 2:17 a.m., the phone rang. Nobody calls the Bureau at 2:17. Nobody calls the Bureau at all. That’s sort of the whole problem.

A shaky voice: Detective… it’s gone. The Spark. The thing that makes every workshop work. Somebody took it.

You’re the new recruit. Your desk is already covered in someone else’s coffee. Grab your coat.

🔦

Chapter 2 · The scene

Three Clues

The room is a mess. Papers everywhere. But three things don’t belong. Tap each one to inspect it.

🧦 One lonely sock

Still warm. Only one. Whoever was here left in a hurry, and in mismatched footwear.

🔑 A key to nothing

It fits no lock in the building. It does, however, fit your pocket suspiciously well.

✏️ A chewed pen cap

The pen is missing. So is every pen in the office. This is bigger than we thought.

Three clues. Three suspects. Time to ask questions.

🕵️😴🌀🖊️

Chapter 3 · Interrogation

Who Do You Question?

Three suspects wait in the interview room. You only have time for one. Choose.

Procrastination yawns: “Spark? I’ll look for it. Tomorrow. Maybe the day after.” Alibi: asleep. Verdict: suspiciously relaxed.

The Dryer rumbles: “I only tumble. Things disappear in here all the time. Ask the socks.” Alibi: spinning. Verdict: hiding a lot.

That One Colleague smiles: “I was just borrowing it.” They’re holding six pens. Alibi: none. Verdict: classic.

Everyone has an alibi. Everyone is lying a little. But something doesn’t add up…

✨

Chapter 4 · The twist

Check Your Pocket

The key fits your pocket. The sock matches your left foot. The pen cap is… yours.

👖 Reach into your pocket

You pull out something small and glowing. The Spark. It was never stolen. You put it down, years ago, and forgot where.

The case was never about a thief. It was about someone who stopped looking. What do you do?

🎉 ✨ 🎊 💫 🎉Ending A. The Spark flares in your hand. The lights come back on. Somewhere, a pen reappears. You are officially a Bureau detective.

Ending B. You leave it on the desk and step outside. The Spark flickers, patient. It knows where the next workshop is. It will wait. It’s very good at waiting.
